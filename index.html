What this measures

Five real barriers: how strict the ID law is, how long people wait at polls, how many households lack internet for online registration, language access, and how many polling places are available relative to the population.

Where the data comes from

ID laws from the National Conference of State Legislatures (2025). Wait times and polling access from MIT's Elections Performance Index (2024 election). Internet and language data from the US Census Bureau's American Community Survey (2024).

Why this exists

The SAVE Act failed in the Senate in June 2026, but that was never really the end of it. Similar bills keep getting reintroduced, and states like Wyoming, Ohio, and South Dakota have already passed their own versions into law. The push to make voting harder, through more paperwork, stricter ID requirements, and fewer options, is ongoing. Over 21 million Americans don't have easy access to a passport or birth certificate. This site is here to make sure you're not one of them.
