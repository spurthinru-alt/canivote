What this measures

Five indicators: state ID requirements, average polling-place wait time, household internet access, language access, and polling-place availability relative to population.

Data sources and years

ID-law information: National Conference of State Legislatures (2025). Wait times and polling access: MIT Elections Performance Index (2024 election). Internet and language measures: US Census Bureau American Community Survey (2024). Source review: June 2026.

Scoring and limits

Composite values are pre-calculated in this file; its source does not document how the five measures are weighted. Composite color bands are 38+ (higher barriers), 28–37 (moderate), 20–27 (some), and below 20 (fewer). Other measures use their own cutoffs. Treat the index as a comparison aid, not a complete picture of voting access.

Why this exists

Voting rules and practical access vary across states. This index brings several measures together and points people toward state-specific resources so they can explore the barriers that may affect them.
